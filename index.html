ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making Demand Forecasting More Intelligent

DemandIQ is a machine learning based demand forecasting application designed to help businesses estimate future product demand.

The system considers important factors such as product category, region, price, discount, promotion, weather, seasonality and competitor pricing.
